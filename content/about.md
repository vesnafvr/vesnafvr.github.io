# About Me

I am a security researcher and PhD student at Dartmouth College advised by Sergey Bratus and Christophe Hauser within the Binary Analysis and Systems Security (BASS) group.

## Research Interests

At the moment, I am interested in researching attack graphs and cloud security. Currently I am working on an agentic cloud attack graph traversal using Datalog. I am also interested in machine learning security, specifically data poisoning. Check out my talks on data poisoning and data poisoning visualization.
